# USER_INTERVIEWS

Three real user interviews are required and should happen early enough to change the product.

## Interview plan

- Interview 1: founder or solo builder paying for at least one AI coding tool
- Interview 2: engineering manager at a small team using multiple AI subscriptions
- Interview 3: technical operator or researcher using mixed AI tools

## Questions to ask

- What AI tools do you or your team pay for right now?
- Do you know your approximate monthly AI spend?
- Have you ever felt like you were paying for overlapping tools?
- What would make an audit credible enough to trust?
- Would you share a public spend-audit result page with others?
