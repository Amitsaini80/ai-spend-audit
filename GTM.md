# GTM

Drafting begins on Day 1 so product positioning evolves alongside implementation rather than after it.

## Early target user hypothesis

The strongest initial target user appears to be an engineering manager or technical founder at a seed to Series A startup with 5 to 40 employees, already paying for multiple AI tools without a clear owner of tooling efficiency.

## Research questions to validate

- Which roles actually notice AI tool overspend first?
- What triggers them to audit spend?
- Which online communities are realistic acquisition channels?
- What messaging sounds credible rather than salesy?
